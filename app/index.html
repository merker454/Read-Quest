Our Mission

At Read Quest, we believe that reading is a journey, not a destination. Our mission is to make reading engaging, rewarding, and fun for readers of all ages and reading levels.

We've created a gamified platform that transforms reading into an adventure where every chapter completed earns points, unlocks achievements, and builds reading streaks. Whether you're a parent encouraging your child's early reading, a student exploring classic literature, or an adult discovering new worlds through books, Read Quest is designed to celebrate your reading journey.

What We Believe:

  • 📚 Reading opens doors to infinite possibilities
  • ✨ Every reader deserves personalized recommendations
  • 🎮 Gamification makes reading more enjoyable and rewarding
  • 🌍 Books connect us across cultures and generations
  • 🏆 Every achievement, no matter how small, deserves celebration

Join us on this quest to foster a love of reading in communities everywhere.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Read Quest is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our reading application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Profile information (name and avatar emoji selection)
  • Reading level selection
  • Book selections and reading progress
  • Points, achievements, and reading streaks

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and maintain your reading profile
  • Provide personalized book recommendations
  • Track reading progress and achievements
  • Improve the app experience

3. Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using browser storage. We do not transmit personal data to external servers. Your reading journey remains private and under your control.

4. Offline Functionality

Read Quest works offline. Service workers cache app data to provide a seamless experience without internet connection.

5. Children's Privacy

Read Quest is designed for readers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents and guardians control their children's profiles.

6. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or questions, please reach out through our Contact page.
